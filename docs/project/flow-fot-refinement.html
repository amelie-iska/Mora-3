The fixed condition is never edited. A subtree correction replaces that node and all its descendants; the sibling branch is preserved. Replacements receive new identities and fresh verification.

What is being measured?

The fixture score is the mean of exp(−z₁²) over every non-condition node. It rewards a simple analytic property, not truth or biological feasibility. The second coordinate retains alternative values. Tokenization in this fixture is numerical quantization, not Mora-3's unfinished trained token-forest decoder.

Production use requires source-bound model, score and decoder implementations. Unknown or failed checks do not become successful outputs. A request for three outputs may return fewer when quality or diversity requirements cannot be met. Selection and edits change the distribution: these results are not unmodified on-policy GFlowNet samples.

Method source: Bi et al., Forest-of-Thought, ICML 2025, sections 3.1–3.3. The paper provides sparse activation, dynamic self-correction and consensus/expert decision-making. Flow-generated latent forests, explicit subtree replacement and a multi-output result API are Mora-3 adaptations.
