Campaign

Transfer settings across model widths.

Maximal update parameterization (μP) is the proposed method for checking whether useful settings at proxy width carry to wider models. Depth, data mixture, exposure, precision, and regularization stay fixed during the first width study. The scope is a roughly 10M-parameter proxy and a target on the order of 70–80M parameters, subject to the final all-head count and resource profile.

Readiness gates

What has to happen first.

Complete corpus and model acceptance

Both owners must confirm the same frozen expanded corpus, eligible rights, audited group/split rules, and native model consumption.

Qualify the exact width family

Enumerate width-scaled and finite axes, tied weights and unique parameters. Check initialization, coordinate behavior, optimizer groups, and exact resume.

Profile resources and recovery

Measure actual full-path memory and throughput, including context length and checkpoint recovery. Profile both the complete proxy and target.

Run proxy experiments

Proposed grid: 3 learning rates × 2 readout multipliers × 2 attention-key multipliers × 2 seeds. No additional standard-parameterization baseline runs are allocated.

Freeze and transfer

Select on a predeclared validation score, then hold selected settings fixed while evaluating across widths. Reserve official test sets for final assessment.

Report all outcomes

Keep failures, early stops, resource limits, exposure, per-task scores, and seed variation. The two target full-training runs occupy the final two slots.
