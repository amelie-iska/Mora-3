Research question

Which changes count as repair?

The track links molecular or electrical interventions to baseline injury, tissue, morphology, expression, and function. Candidate goals might include remodeling, regrowth, or functional restoration. A candidate design must state which endpoint it targets and what evidence supports that link.

Design and evaluation

Rank testable interventions.

Condition inverse design on the baseline, a desired outcome, and admissible action families. Keep predicted morphology and expression separate from functional evidence. Independent controls and longitudinal observations are needed to tell repair from transient marker changes, remodeling, or selective cell survival.

Cellular senescence measurements may motivate a separate hypothesis track. A shift in a senescence marker does not establish rejuvenation, tissue restoration, or lifespan extension.
