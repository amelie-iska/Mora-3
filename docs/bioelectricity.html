Research question

Can a perturbation redirect a biological state?

The track models ion channels, pumps, gap junctions, membrane potential, and spatial coupling alongside lineage, developmental stage, injury state, and time. The forward task predicts a declared observed response. The inverse task proposes intervention hypotheses given a desired state and baseline.

Qualification

Cell fate needs lineage evidence.

Separately score cellular identity conversion, selective survival or expansion, shape rearrangement, and tissue function. A marker shift alone cannot identify a fate conversion. The plan needs lineage tracking, orthogonal identity markers, control arms, and persistence over follow-up. Study, species, and assay holdouts are separate where supported.
