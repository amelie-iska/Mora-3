Research question

Will a candidate bind in the intended context?

The planned tasks connect ligand or binder identity, target sequence or isoform, structural context, and native assay outcomes. The model could score a measured binding endpoint in the forward direction or propose constrained candidates for a desired target profile in the inverse direction.
