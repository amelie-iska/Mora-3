Evidence standards

What would make a hypothesis useful?

Start with a source-linked, target-free baseline and stated desired endpoint. Propose only candidates compatible with the request’s constraints and preserve uncertainty, alternative explanations, and failed checks. Candidate generation and predictive scoring are separate from confirmation in an independent assay.

All four tracks describe planned research capabilities. The site reports no intervention efficacy, validated clinical outcomes, or trained benchmarks.
