# Source-bound joint forest supervision

For a source-known finite forest skeleton S, fixed source context c, clean latent target y, independent Gaussian x0 and uniform t in [0,1), the existing conditional path is x_t = [1-(1-sigma_min)t]x0 + ty on available free coordinates. Its velocity target is u_t = y-(1-sigma_min)x0. Unavailable coordinates retain noise and known observations are clamped. For positive sigma_min the terminal conditional path retains residual noise; it is not falsely described as an exact delta at y.

L_flow is the mean squared velocity error over available coordinates. L_token is complete-prefix categorical cross-entropy on a separate detached source-encoder export with exactly one final stop token. L_quality is the sum of available whole-forest and node criterion binary cross-entropies divided by their available count. L_analogy is the query mean of logsumexp over known, group-disjoint pairs minus logsumexp over supported positive pairs, with the existing declared temperature. The objective is L = sum_j w_j L_j for nonnegative fixed w_j; absent terms are omitted without renormalizing the remaining weights. A batch with no active supervision has no optimization loss.

All four heads share the same actual native field and geometry/TokenGT parameters. Data preflight precedes every forward and random draw. Source-known topology, labels, latent export alignment, masks and source rights require independent qualification. A finite segment and exact source cursor are bound to the trial and checkpoint; changed source/operator declarations or invalid positions refuse before state copy. This interface adds no learned parameters and grants no dataset or model admission.

The accompanying CPU tests compare original per-head equations and gradients, preserve target exclusions and unknown labels, and reproduce the next objective from a zero-update native checkpoint. They do not establish trained model accuracy, causal validity, reward-proportional sampled forests, completed-update numerical recovery, or full-corpus readiness. Unknown-topology policy learning, full native source materialization and the counted 24 + 2 campaign remain distinct unfinished requirements. See docs/forest_training_integration_20261113.md for executable interfaces and scope.

Reference: Lipman et al., Flow Matching for Generative Modeling, arXiv:2210.02747v2. The native joint objective and checkpoint/source composition are Mora-3 adaptations.
