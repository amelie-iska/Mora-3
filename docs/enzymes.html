Constraints

A geometric match has a scope.

Separate transition-state internal geometry, docking pose after protein alignment, and the enzyme template’s mapped subset. A caller-supplied RMSD limit applies only to its selected references and atom or residue mapping. A missing reference prevents a measured pass.

Planned exports include XYZ with atom mapping, enzyme FASTA, and complex mmCIF. Validate on reloading the files. Preserve connectivity, stereochemistry, cofactors and uncertainty where they matter.

Qualification

Separate geometry from catalysis.

Computed transition states, analogues, predicted complexes, and experimentally observed enzyme activity are distinct evidence. Hold out related reactions, protein families, catalytic motifs, complexes, and templates. A structure match does not establish a catalytic mechanism or activity.
