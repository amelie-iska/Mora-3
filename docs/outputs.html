Output register

What a result would contain.

Output familyIntended artifactEvidence and context to retain
Sequences and molecular graphsCandidate proteins, RNA, small molecules, or editsSequence or structure-aware representation; check chemistry, mapping, novelty, and diversity.
3D coordinates and complexesCandidate structures, poses, and transition statesCoordinates plus reference and atom map; validate geometry, chirality, and structural constraints.
Cellular responsesExpression changes, phenotype, and pathway or regulatory readoutsUnits and context, with likelihood or uncertainty appropriate to the observed endpoint.
Images and segmentationGenerated/predicted images, cell masks, and spatial measurementsLinked raster, instance, and feature representations; compare with independent masks or images.
Native scalars and bindingAssay-specific quantities with bounds or censoringPreserve endpoint, assay method, units, direction, and missingness.
Ranked hypothesesFeasible candidates, tradeoffs, provenance, or refusalScore alongside limitations, prediction uncertainty, and independent evidence where available.

ArtifactStructure with atom mapping
VerifyReload; check RMSD, connectivity and stereo
ReportMapping, tolerance, and missing references

Geometry compliance does not establish catalysis, binding, or efficacy.

ArtifactNative response and uncertainty
VerifyIndependent held-out endpoint
ReportUnits, error, calibration, and coverage

Evaluate each endpoint on its own scale and document where the model should abstain.

ArtifactRanked hypotheses and alternatives
VerifyValidity, constraints, and independent support
ReportTradeoffs and unsupported cases

Keep soft scoring preferences separate from hard feasibility requirements.
