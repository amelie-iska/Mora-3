## Original train census and preserved supervision

The original NIH 2014 challenge Tox21 release (content pinned 2026-10-31) has **51 original train Arrow shards** containing **12,892 directional tasks**, i.e. **6,446 independent source molecule records mirrored in forward and inverse roles**, not 12,892 independent chemical experiments. All original source shard fingerprints, group sidecars, record IDs, native Arrow bytes, task payload digests, source revisions and exact ordered source ordinals were authenticated. Both snapshot iterators exhausted every eligible original training record. **200 independent cross-shard cursor restarts** verified continuity (50 boundaries × two directions × two source bindings). The forward cursor terminates at the final eligible forward ordinal within shard 50, while the inverse cursor reaches `[51,0]`; the first red test uncovered and corrected this important sparse-direction cursor distinction. Neither iterator silently consumed the other direction as a training example.

The original **12 named assay axes** were preserved in order, without converting unknown measurements to negative labels. Across 6,446 original molecules and 77,352 source assay positions, 3,338 were observed positive, 49,929 observed negative, and **24,085 genuinely unknown/masked**. Forward supervision contains 53,267 observed positions; inverse molecule generation prepares 243,133 complete native codec token positions, including original target termination semantics. Original forward and inverse role-mirrored SMILES, call masks, binary values, chemical groups and source-ref identities matched exactly. The provider's biological assay calls are *source-reported*; physical dose, time, cell-line context and independently validated causal effects are not supplied. The original source test/validation roles and target values were neither decoded nor enrolled as a model selection reference.
