Cooperation

Segmentation can inform both directions.

Where records support it, cell segmentation is meant to turn images into object masks, spatial graphs, and morphology features. That can support image-to-measurement tasks or use a desired shape as a condition. Agreement between a predicted image and its mask is an internal check; it does not replace an independent observed result.
